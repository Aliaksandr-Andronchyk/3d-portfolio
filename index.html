Pipeline

From prompt to a running iPhone scene

Three stages, each with a hard budget and a check that has to pass before the asset moves on.

01

Generation — Hunyuan3D-2 and Tripo

Concept image in, dense mesh out. Hunyuan3D-2 runs on a remote GPU box and produces the props you can orbit above, at one to two million triangles each. Tripo handles the character and the interface panels, where a cleaner topology matters more than raw surface detail.

02

Retopology and rigging — Blender

Headless Blender does the unglamorous half: decimation to budget, normals, UVs, then Auto-Rig Pro for the skeleton and Mixamo retargeting for the motion library. The chef lands at 41 bones and 7 clips; props are cut to ≤1k triangles for the app build, 180k for these web viewers.

03

Delivery — USDZ for SceneKit

Every asset exports to USDZ per clip and is verified by loading it into SceneKit and counting node.animationKeys — not by eyeballing it in Blender. Budgets are enforced, not hoped for: ≤100k triangles for the character, ≤25 MB per USDZ, and the four-state interface rig ships at 336 KB in total.

Honest boundaries. The props on this page are generation output cleaned up for the web — they are not hand-modelled, and the decimation shows if you zoom far enough. The character is the one asset taken all the way through rigging, animation and on-device delivery.
